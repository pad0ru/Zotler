1. Extract Data: Log into your student portal and navigate to "Degree Works." On top left, there will be a printer icon. Use that to download your classes.
2. Process with AI: Paste the copied data into an AI tool (such as Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, or DeepSeek). In a separate tab, copy and paste the required prompt into the same AI session.
3. Export CSV: Download the degreeworks.csv file exported by the AI, then import it into Zotler in Settings under Courses. If the AI cannot provide a downloadable file, save its raw CSV output as degreeworks.csv.
